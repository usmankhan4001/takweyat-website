1. "About Us" Page: The Civil Society Coalition
Introduction
Headline: A Coalition of Professionals. A Commitment to Society. Body Text: Takweyat
International Organization (تقویت) is a dynamic collective of civil society members originating
from diverse professional backgrounds. We are lawyers, doctors, educators, and social workers
who believe that true societal strength comes from active, on-ground participation rather than
passive observation. Grounded in core Islamic values of justice, ethical responsibility, and
brotherhood, we pool our own professional expertise and time to intervene where society needs
it most. Whether it is providing pro-bono legal defense, setting up medical camps, or executing
disaster relief, Takweyat is driven purely by the power of human capital and community
dedication.
Our Structure: The Power of Civil Society
Text: We are not a traditional charity; we are a multidisciplinary movement. Our main
organizational body is composed of respected members of civil society who volunteer their
specialized skills to create systemic, long-term change. By uniting different professions under
one banner, we ensure that every challenge—from human rights violations to climate
disasters—is met with expert, actionable solutions.
Founder’s Message
Message: "Throughout my career in the legal field, I have witnessed the devastating impact of
systemic neglect. I also realized that the solution lies within us—the professionals and active
members of civil society. Takweyat (تقویت) was born from the realization that we cannot wait for
others to fix our society. We do not ask for your funds; we ask for your expertise. From fighting
human rights violations in the courtroom to delivering medical aid on the streets, our
commitment is fueled by the personal dedication of our members. I invite professionals from all
walks of life to join us in this mission of empathy, justice, and actionable change."
2. "Get Involved" & Professional Contribution Portal
(This entirely replaces the Donation Portal. It focuses strictly on recruiting human resources and
professional services.)
Headline: We Don’t Ask for Funds. We Ask for Your Expertise. Sub-text: Takweyat operates on
the strength of its people. We are actively seeking driven professionals, field workers, and
subject-matter experts who want to contribute their time and skills to our active missions.
How You Can Contribute (The Volunteer Hub)
Users can click on the category that matches their background to sign up:
1. The Legal Network (Advocates & Law Students):
○ Contribute by taking on pro-bono cases, drafting public interest petitions, or
conducting legal awareness seminars in marginalized communities.
2. The Medical & Healthcare Network (Doctors & Nurses):

○ Dedicate a few hours a month to our free medical camps or provide free
consultations to critical patients referred by our teams.
3. The Education & Mentorship Panel (Teachers & Corporate Professionals):
○ Visit middle-class schools and colleges with us to provide modern career
counseling, ethical training, and digital literacy workshops.
4. The Field Action & Relief Squad (Youth & Civil Society):
○ Be the boots on the ground during natural disasters (floods, earthquakes), or help
organize and distribute food during our Ramadan drives and daily meal initiatives.
5. In-Kind & Institutional Partnerships (Businesses & Organizations):
○ We welcome businesses that wish to contribute directly to the cause—such as
bakeries providing food directly for our drives, or printing presses providing
educational materials for our awareness campaigns.
