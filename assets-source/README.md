# assets-source

Raw files supplied by the owner. Not served by the site and excluded from the Docker build.

- `icon-set-nonprofit/` Nonprofit organisation icon set from Envato: 16 subjects (Animal Shelter, Book Donation, Community, Disaster Relief, Donation Jar, Dove, Education Support, Fundraiser, Global Aid, Helping Hand, Medical Aid, Megaphone, Orphan Care, Social Impact, Volunteer, Water Charity) in Dypta (duotone), Line and Solid, colour and black, as SVG/PNG/EPS/AI. Original colours navy `#293a4c` and orange `#ff5f00`. The recoloured duotone SVGs (deep teal `#00504F` + raspberry `#A21E66`) used by the site are in `../public/icons/`. Animal Shelter and Fundraiser do not fit the organisation.
- `hands-video/` "Symbolic human hands reaching towards each other" from Envato: `helping hand Color.mov` and `helping hand Alpha.mov`, ProRes 1080p, 10 s. Convert with ffmpeg to an H.264 MP4 loop (silent, about 1 to 2 MB) before using.

The two Envato background videos used on the site were compressed from files in the owner's Downloads folder (`islamabad-with-the-faisal-mosque-...mp4`, `scales-of-justice-gavel-and-law-books-...mov`); the compressed results are in `../public/media/`.
