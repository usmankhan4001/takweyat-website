# 01 Project brief

## The organisation
**Takweyat International Organization** (تقویت). The documents call it that; the logo graphic still reads "Foundation" (owner was asked which name is right; open). A coalition of professionals (lawyers, doctors, educators, social workers) who give their own time and skill. Grounded in Islamic values of justice, ethical responsibility and brotherhood. It does **not** ask for money: "We do not ask for your funds; we ask for your expertise." Members are barred from soliciting donations.

Offices: Head Office, Islamabad (Bahria Town Phase 3). Regional Legal Chamber, Gujar Khan (Chamber Number 3, Basement, Lawyers Complex Building, Kutchery). Email contact@takweyat.org. Phone, WhatsApp and legal aid helpline are not supplied yet (lines appear automatically when filled in `src/data/site.ts`).

Work areas (7): Fighting Hunger, Ramadan & Spiritual Activities, Disaster Relief, Free Legal Aid, Education & Career Counseling, Medical Camps, Community Support.
Five pillars: Educational Reforms & Youth Empowerment; Hunger Eradication & Spiritual Enrichment; Human Rights Protection & Social Justice; Disaster Management & Climate Change; Healthcare Access & Medical Security.
Two long-term plans: **The Educational Plan: The Global Vision** (35 pillars, 4 phases) and **The Global Legal Aid and Justice Plan: The Manifesto of Rights** (50 pillars, 4 phases). Fourteen initiatives (Legal Chambers, Boycott Israel Platform, Publishing House, schools, colleges, open university, teacher academy, library, vocational institutes, startup incubator, learner app, two funds, global legal coalition).
Five volunteer networks (Legal; Medical & Healthcare; Education & Mentorship Panel; Field Action & Relief Squad; In-Kind & Institutional Partnerships).

## Purpose of the website
1. Explain who Takweyat is and what it does, quickly.
2. Let people who cannot afford a lawyer ask for free legal aid (the most urgent action).
3. Recruit professional volunteers and partners; give the press a desk.
4. Publish the two long-term plans in depth (web pages and designed PDF whitepapers).
5. Link to the live Boycott Israel Platform (https://boycottisraelonline.com, currently a status page).

## Owner and working style
The owner is the founder-level decision maker, writes short informal messages, and reacts strongly to anything that looks generic, cartoonish, heavy or text-dense. They give inspiration by example (https://www.farmafrica.org/ for look, motion and fonts; bdsmovement.net for an earlier status page) and approve visually. They supply media (Envato account, ChatGPT image generation, Figma illustration set) and want to review designs in an artifact or the live preview before they are applied. When they say "lets discuss" they want a discussion and recommendation first, not a build.

## Inputs
Six source documents (see `../../source-documents/README.md`). Aesthetic reference: farmafrica.org (pinned scroll hero that zooms into a letter, colour-wipe buttons, slide-up reveals, big editorial type).
