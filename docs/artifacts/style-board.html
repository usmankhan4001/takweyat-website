Takweyat International Organization · Website outlook

One teal. One raspberry. Calm paper. Light type.

The content stays exactly as it is. This board shows how the outlook changes: a single colour system, buttons that follow the surface they sit on, Phosphor icons, an animated call-to-action, and lighter type.

Everything below is live, so hover, tab and scroll. Nothing on the website has been changed yet.

1 · Colour

Six colours, each with one job

The yellowy tones are gone. The warm cream paper becomes a cool, slightly green-tinted off-white, and the olive field colour is dropped from the screen entirely. It can stay in print and in the logo family if you want it.

3 · The animated call-to-action

One button, five asks, rolling

The header button and the mobile bar cycle through the five ways to take part. The words roll up like a departures board, then settle. The destination changes with the word. Hover or focus pauses it, and with reduced motion it shows the first label only.

4 · Type weight

Less bold, more light

Gloock is already a heavy face, so it stays at its single weight and carries the headings. Bold on top of it is what looked loud. Menu, buttons and labels drop from 600 or 700 to 500, and body text stays at 400.
