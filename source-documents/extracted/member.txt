Section: Join the Movement (Membership & Conduct)
Headline: Excellence in Character. Dedication in Action. Body Text: Takweyat (تﯾوﻘﺗ) is built on
the integrity and expertise of its members. Because we operate directly with vulnerable
communities, handle sensitive legal and medical matters, and represent the best of civil society,
we hold our members to the highest ethical and professional standards. Joining us means
making a serious commitment to societal upliftment.
1. Membership Criteria: Who Can Join?
We do not ask for membership fees, but we do demand dedication. To join the Takweyat
network, individuals must meet the following criteria:
● Professional or Civic Background: Applicants should be professionals (lawyers,
doctors, educators, corporate employees), skilled workers, or dedicated students willing to
offer their specific expertise.
● Ethical Alignment: A firm belief in justice, equality, and the core Islamic values of
brotherhood, empathy, and social responsibility.
● Action-Oriented Mindset: Membership is not a mere title or a resume addition. Members
must be willing to dedicate actual time and physical effort—whether that means fighting a
pro-bono case, spending a weekend at a medical camp, or distributing food during
Ramadan.
● Clean Record: Members must possess a strong moral character with no history of
professional misconduct or criminal convictions.
2. Code of Conduct: Our Operational Ethics
Every volunteer, member, and partner acting under the banner of Takweyat is bound by this
Code of Conduct.
● Absolute Confidentiality (Legal & Medical): Many who seek our help are in vulnerable
situations. Our legal and medical volunteers must maintain strict attorney-client and
doctor-patient confidentiality. The dignity and privacy of those we help are non-negotiable.
● Zero Financial Solicitation: Takweyat is a coalition of civil society, not a fundraising
agency. Members are strictly prohibited from soliciting, collecting, or managing financial
donations from the public in the name of the organization. All contributions are made
through personal time, professional expertise, or direct in-kind support.
● Apolitical & Non-Partisan Stance: We serve humanity, not political parties. Takweyat
operates independently of all political organizations. Members must not use the platform
to promote political agendas or engage in partisan activism.
● Equality and Respect: We assist everyone regardless of their background, status, or
belief. Members must treat all beneficiaries, colleagues, and community members with
absolute respect, patience, and compassion.
● Commitment to Safety: Whether navigating a flood disaster zone or visiting a remote
school, members must adhere to the safety guidelines set by the core team to protect
themselves and those they are helping.
● Representation: Members must remember that their public behavior reflects on the
entire organization. Professionalism in speech, attire, and conduct is expected at all times
when representing Takweyat.

The Onboarding Process
1. Application: Submit your professional details and area of interest via our Contact Form.
2. Review: Our core committee reviews the application to match your skills with our current
on-ground needs.
3. Orientation: Selected volunteers undergo a brief orientation regarding our operational
ethics and current projects before their first deployment.
