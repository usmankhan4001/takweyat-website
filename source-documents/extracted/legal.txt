1. Scope of Cases We Take
To manage public expectations, clearly define the types of cases your network accepts.
● Criminal Defense for the Marginalized: Representation for innocent, low-income
individuals facing wrongful accusations, illegal detention, or those unable to afford a
lawyer for under-trial proceedings.
● Human Rights & Constitutional Violations: Fighting cases of unlawful harassment,
exploitation, missing persons, or severe violations of fundamental citizen rights by state or
private actors.
● Domestic Violence & Family Protection: Immediate legal support for victims of
domestic abuse, including protection orders, maintenance, and child custody support for
vulnerable women and orphans.
● Public Interest Litigation (PIL): Challenging unjust laws, systemic corruption, or
environmental hazards (like climate negligence) that affect the broader community or
vulnerable populations.
● Note: We do not accept commercial, corporate, or property/land disputes unless they
involve the direct exploitation of a highly vulnerable individual.
2. Eligibility Criteria
Establish clear boundaries to ensure the service reaches those who genuinely need it. Text:
Our resources and volunteer hours are dedicated strictly to those in extreme need. To qualify for
Takweyat’s free legal aid, applicants must meet the following criteria:
1. Financial Inability: Must be unable to afford standard legal fees (proof of income or a
genuine declaration of financial hardship is required).
2. Merit of the Case: The case must involve a genuine violation of rights, a threat to liberty,
or severe exploitation.
3. Vulnerability Status: Priority is given to widows, orphans, minorities, under-trial prisoners
without representation, and victims of extreme physical or mental abuse.

3. How to Apply (The 4-Step Process)
A simple roadmap so users know exactly what to expect after they reach out.
1. Initial Submission: Fill out our confidential legal aid form below or contact our dedicated
helpline.
2. Merit & Eligibility Review: Our screening committee (comprising senior advocates) will
review the facts of your case and your financial eligibility within 48 to 72 hours.
3. Confidential Consultation: If approved, you will be invited to our Islamabad Head Office
or our Gujar Khan Legal Chamber for a secure, face-to-face consultation.
4. Legal Action: A dedicated volunteer advocate will be assigned to draft your petition, file
your case, or represent you in court—completely free of charge.
