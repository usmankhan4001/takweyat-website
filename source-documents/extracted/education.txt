TAKWEYAT (تﯾوﻘﺗ) EDUCATIONAL PLAN:
THE GLOBAL VISION
Executive Summary
Education is not merely the transfer of information; it is the architectural foundation of a just,
equitable, and progressive society. For Takweyat (تﯾوﻘﺗ), education is the ultimate equalizer. This
masterplan outlines a monumental, multi-generational initiative designed to completely overhaul
the educational paradigm. We seek to protect history, unveil the truth behind global narratives,
empower citizens with legal and moral rights, and build a technologically advanced, ethically
grounded generation. From constructing physical infrastructure like free hostels and libraries to
launching global digital universities, AI applications, and a premier publishing house, this
framework serves as the blueprint for an educational revolution.
Our Ultimate Goal
To democratize high-quality, ethically rooted, and technologically advanced education, making it
accessible to every individual regardless of their geographic or financial background. We aim to
cultivate critical thinkers who are proud of their mother tongue (Urdu) and heritage, fluent in
future technologies (AI), aware of their constitutional rights, and equipped to contribute to global
social justice.
The Strategic Roadmap (From Pakistan to the World)
● Phase 1: The Grassroots Foundation (Years 1-3): Focus on rural and difficult areas in
Pakistan. Launch the Takweyat Publishing House, initiate Urdu and true-history book
projects, develop basic exam-prep applications, and establish the first tier of free
community libraries and teacher-training workshops.
● Phase 2: Institutional Infrastructure (Years 4-7): Lay the physical and digital
foundations for the Takweyat Chain of Schools and Colleges. Begin construction of free
student hostels, launch comprehensive EdTech portals, and integrate legal/moral values
into localized curricula.
● Phase 3: The Higher Education & Tech Leap (Years 8-10): Establish the Takweyat
Global Open University (TGOU) for worldwide enrollment. Fully integrate AI and Asian
language courses, and deploy advanced educational apps for holistic daily life and
academic management.
● Phase 4: Global Expansion & Policy Leadership (Years 11+): Expand the chain of
institutions internationally. Partner with global educationists, export the Takweyat ethical
curriculum, and establish our platforms as the authoritative voice on history, human rights,
and educational philosophy worldwide.
The 35 Pillars of Educational Transformation

1. The Educational Vision & Philosophy
● Establish a holistic curriculum that balances modern scientific inquiry with deep-rooted

● Shift the focus from rote memorization to critical thinking, problem-solving, and analytical

● Foster a culture of lifelong learning where students view education as a continuous
journey, not just a path to a degree.
● Ensure that every educational policy drafted by Takweyat is strictly student-centric and
adaptable to individual learning paces.
● Promote intellectual curiosity by rewarding questioning and debate within the classroom
environment.
● Integrate the concept of Tarbiyah (nurturing character) alongside Ta'leem (imparting
knowledge).
● Eliminate the commercialization of basic education, advocating for it as a fundamental,
non-negotiable human right.
● Create an inclusive environment that respects diverse thoughts while anchoring students
in strong moral principles.
● Regularly audit educational outcomes not by test scores, but by the societal impact and
ethical conduct of the graduates.
